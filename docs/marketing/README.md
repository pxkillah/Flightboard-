# Marketing kit: Car Report and Flightboard

Everything needed to find users on social media. Start once each app is live in the stores.

- [`calendar.md`](calendar.md): 4 weeks of posts, with a script for every short video.
- [`posts.md`](posts.md): ready-to-adapt posts for Reddit and Facebook groups.
- This page: the weekly routine, the rules, and how to tell what's working.

## Who each app is for

| | Car Report | Flightboard |
|---|---|---|
| People | Used-car buyers, car owners keeping records, new drivers, mechanics | Travellers, people picking someone up at the airport, aviation fans |
| Their problem | "Is this car safe? Does it have a recall?" / "Where are my service records?" | "Is my flight on time? Which gate? Which baggage belt?" |
| Hook | Free recalls that dealers fix at no cost | The airport board in your pocket, plus a live radar |
| Language | English and French (Quebec) | English |

## The weekly routine (about 3 hours a week)

1. **Monday:** film the week's videos in one go (about 15 minutes each, on your phone).
2. **Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday:** post one video, the same clip on TikTok, Instagram
   Reels and YouTube Shorts. Mid-afternoon or evening tends to work better than mornings.
3. **Every day, 15 minutes:** answer comments, and answer 2–3 questions in Reddit or
   Facebook groups (see `posts.md`).
4. **Sunday:** check the numbers (below) and note which video did best. Make more like it.

## Rules that keep the accounts and apps safe

- **Be honest about what the app does.** Car Report shows recalls by model, owner
  complaints and crash ratings. It does **not** show accident, lien or ownership history:
  never call it a Carfax replacement. Say "free recall and safety check".
- **Don't use other companies' names or logos** in ads or app keywords (Carfax, airline
  logos, FlightAware and so on). Mentioning them in a sentence that compares honestly is fine.
- **Never buy or swap reviews**, and don't ask for 5 stars. Both stores remove apps for it.
  The app asks for a rating on its own after someone has saved three records.
- **Say "I made this"** whenever you share your own app in a community. Read each group's
  self-promotion rules first, and never paste the same post into many groups.
- **Film safely and legally:** don't show licence plates, people's faces without asking, or
  the inside of a dealership without permission. Use your own car or a friend's.
- **Never film while driving.**

## Telling what's working

Both stores show where downloads came from, with no tracking code in the apps:

- App Store Connect → **App Analytics** → Acquisition → Campaigns.
- Play Console → **Statistics**, filtered by UTM campaign (or Store performance → Traffic sources).

Use a different link for each platform, so you can see which one brings people. Fill in
the two IDs once the apps are live:

- **App Store:** in App Store Connect → App Analytics → Acquisition → Campaigns, choose
  "Generate a Campaign Link". It looks like
  `https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id<APP_ID>?pt=<PROVIDER_ID>&ct=<campaign>&mt=8`.
- **Google Play:**
  `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pxkillah.carreport&referrer=utm_source%3D<campaign>%26utm_medium%3Dsocial`

| Where | Campaign name to use |
|---|---|
| TikTok bio link | `tiktok` |
| Instagram bio link | `instagram` |
| YouTube Shorts description | `youtube` |
| Reddit | `reddit` |
| Facebook groups | `facebook` |
| QR code at a garage or car lot | `qr-<shop name>` |

Most short-video apps allow one link in the bio. Use a page that shows both store links
(for example on the same site as `privacy.html`), and give that page's two buttons the
campaign links above.

## What to watch each Sunday

| Number | Where | Good sign |
|---|---|---|
| Views and saves per video | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube | A video saved or shared a lot is a topic to repeat |
| Product page views | App Store Connect, Play Console | Rising with your posts |
| Conversion (page views → downloads) | Same | Above 25–30% means the screenshots and text work |
| Ratings | Both stores | Answer every low rating politely, with how you fixed it |

## When to spend money

Only after a month of posting shows which message works and the app has some ratings.
Then try **Apple Search Ads** with a small budget ($5–10 a day for two weeks), on searches
like "vin check", "car recall" and "vin decoder". It reaches people already looking for
this. Stop anything that doesn't bring downloads within two weeks.
