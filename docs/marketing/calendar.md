# 4-week posting calendar

**Car Report:** 3 short videos a week (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday). Post each in English
and, a day later, in French. Use separate English and French accounts if you can, because
TikTok shows videos to people by language.
**Flightboard:** 1 video a week (Sunday), starting once it's in the stores.

Every video is vertical (9:16), 15–30 seconds, filmed on your phone, with on-screen text
(many people watch without sound). Record the phone screen with iPhone's Screen Recording
(Control Centre) or Android's Screen recorder, and film the car with the camera.

Each script has:
- **Hook:** the first 2 seconds, which decide whether people keep watching.
- **Shots:** what to film.
- **Say:** the voice-over, or on-screen text if you'd rather not talk.
- **Caption:** the text under the video.

End every Car Report video with: *"Car Report. Free on the App Store and Google Play.
Link in bio."* / *« Car Report. Gratuit sur l'App Store et Google Play. Lien dans la bio. »*

## Week 1: "What is this?"

### 1. Check this before you buy a used car (Tuesday)
- **Hook (on screen):** "Do this before you buy ANY used car"
- **Shots:** open the driver's door → close-up of the VIN sticker → tap the scan button in
  Car Report → the report appears → scroll to the recalls.
- **Say:** "Every car has a VIN on the door frame. Scan it with Car Report and you'll see
  every recall for that model, owner complaints and crash ratings. Free, no account."
- **Caption:** Check any car's recalls in 10 seconds 🚗 #usedcars #carbuying #carrecall #carsoftiktok
- **Français.** Accroche : « Faites ça avant d'acheter N'IMPORTE QUELLE auto usagée. »
  Texte : « Chaque véhicule a un NIV dans le cadre de la portière. Scannez-le avec Car Report :
  rappels du modèle, plaintes de propriétaires et cotes de sécurité. Gratuit, sans compte. »
  Légende : Les rappels de n'importe quelle auto en 10 secondes 🚗 #autousagée #rappel #autoquébec

### 2. Your car might have a free repair waiting (Thursday)
- **Hook:** "Your car might have a FREE repair waiting at the dealer"
- **Shots:** your own car → type or scan its VIN → show a recall → open "Risk and remedy".
- **Say:** "Recalls are fixed free at the dealer, but millions of cars never get them done.
  Check yours in Car Report, then call a dealer and ask if it's been done on your VIN."
- **Caption:** Recall repairs are free. Check yours. #carrecall #carowner #savemoney
- **Français.** Accroche : « Votre auto a peut-être une réparation GRATUITE qui l'attend. »
  Texte : « Les rappels sont réparés gratuitement chez le concessionnaire, mais beaucoup
  d'autos ne sont jamais réparées. Vérifiez la vôtre, puis appelez un concessionnaire avec
  votre NIV. » Légende : Les réparations de rappel sont gratuites. #rappel #autoquébec

### 3. Where to find your VIN in 10 seconds (Saturday)
- **Hook:** "3 places your VIN is hiding"
- **Shots:** windshield corner (from outside, driver's side) → door-frame sticker →
  insurance card or registration (blur the name and plate!) → open Car Report's home screen
  showing the same tips.
- **Say:** "Bottom of the windshield, the driver's door frame, and your insurance card.
  17 characters, never the letters I, O or Q."
- **Caption:** Save this for when you need it 📌 #carhacks #vin #newdriver
- **Français.** Accroche : « 3 endroits où se cache votre NIV. » Texte : « Au bas du
  pare-brise, dans le cadre de la portière, et sur votre attestation d'assurance.
  17 caractères, jamais de I, de O ou de Q. » Légende : À garder pour plus tard 📌 #NIV #auto

## Week 2: "Real cars, real results"

### 4. I checked 5 used cars for sale (Tuesday)
- **Hook:** "I checked 5 used cars for sale. Here's what I found."
- **Shots:** screenshots of 5 listings with VINs (many listings show the VIN; blur plates,
  sellers' names and phone numbers) → each one in Car Report → count the recalls.
- **Say:** keep it factual: "This one has 3 recalls, one says don't drive it until it's
  fixed. Ask the seller if it's been done."
- **Caption:** Always ask the seller about open recalls. #usedcars #carbuyingtips
- **Français.** Accroche : « J'ai vérifié 5 autos usagées à vendre. » Texte : « Celle-ci a
  3 rappels, dont un qui dit de ne pas la conduire avant la réparation. Demandez au vendeur. »

### 5. The "do not drive" warning (Thursday)
- **Hook:** "If you see THIS screen, park the car"
- **Shots:** the red "Do not drive" banner in Car Report (use a model with a park-it recall,
  for example from the news) → the recall text.
- **Say:** "Some recalls say don't drive until it's repaired, often for air bags that can
  injure you. Car Report puts that warning at the top."
- **Caption:** Some recalls are serious. Check yours. #carsafety #recall
- **Français.** Accroche : « Si vous voyez CET écran, stationnez l'auto. » Texte : « Certains
  rappels disent de ne pas conduire avant la réparation, souvent pour des coussins gonflables.
  Car Report l'affiche en haut. »

### 6. Not every recall applies to your car (Saturday)
- **Hook:** "That recall might not even be for your car"
- **Shots:** a report where a recall is flagged "probably doesn't apply" → open it → the
  reason (for example "covers all-wheel drive; yours is front-wheel drive").
- **Say:** "Recalls are listed by model, but some only cover one engine or drive type.
  Car Report flags those and tells you why, so you know what to ask the dealer."
- **Caption:** Know which recalls really apply to your car. #carrecall #cartips
- **Français.** Accroche : « Ce rappel ne vise peut-être même pas votre auto. » Texte :
  « Les rappels sont listés par modèle, mais certains visent un seul moteur ou une seule
  traction. Car Report les signale et explique pourquoi. »

## Week 3: "Keep your car's story"

### 7. Stop losing your service receipts (Tuesday)
- **Hook:** "My car's whole service history, in one place"
- **Shots:** a glovebox full of receipts → Car Report's "My records" with the chart → add
  an oil change in 10 seconds.
- **Say:** "Log oil changes, repairs and odometer readings. When you sell, share the
  history with the buyer. It stays on your phone; no account."
- **Caption:** Sell your car for more with a full service history. #carmaintenance #oilchange
- **Français.** Accroche : « Tout l'historique d'entretien de mon auto, au même endroit. »
  Texte : « Notez vidanges, réparations et relevés d'odomètre. À la vente, partagez
  l'historique avec l'acheteur. Ça reste sur votre téléphone. »

### 8. Odometer rollback: how to spot it (Thursday)
- **Hook:** "How sellers fake the mileage, and how to catch it"
- **Shots:** talk to camera, then Car Report's odometer chart with a reading lower than an
  earlier one → the red warning.
- **Say:** "If a reading is ever lower than an earlier one, something's wrong. Car Report
  warns you. For a car you're buying, compare the odometer with old service receipts."
- **Caption:** Mileage should only go up. #usedcars #odometer #scamalert
- **Français.** Accroche : « Comment on trafique l'odomètre, et comment le détecter. »
  Texte : « Si un relevé est plus bas qu'un précédent, il y a un problème. Car Report vous
  avertit. Pour une auto à acheter, comparez avec les vieilles factures d'entretien. »
- **Careful:** this explains what to look for; it doesn't claim Car Report checks a car's
  past mileage from a database (it doesn't).

### 9. Bought a car in the US? Miles and km (Saturday)
- **Hook:** "Car from the States? Your odometer is in miles."
- **Shots:** an odometer in miles → Car Report, add a reading in mi → the chart showing
  km and mi readings together.
- **Say:** "Each reading keeps its own unit, and the app still catches a rollback across
  miles and km. US states and Canadian provinces for registrations, too."
- **Caption:** Built for Canada and the US 🇨🇦🇺🇸 #carimport #crossborder
- **Français.** Accroche : « Une auto des États-Unis? L'odomètre est en milles. » Texte :
  « Chaque relevé garde son unité, et l'application détecte quand même un recul entre milles
  et km. »

## Week 4: "Trust and community"

### 10. Your data stays on your phone (Tuesday)
- **Hook:** "This car app has no account, no ads, no tracking"
- **Shots:** the app in airplane mode showing saved records → the privacy page.
- **Say:** "Your records stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is the VIN you
  check, which goes to the US safety agency NHTSA."
- **Caption:** Privacy shouldn't be a premium feature. #privacy #apps
- **Français.** Accroche : « Une appli auto sans compte, sans pub, sans pistage. » Texte :
  « Vos entrées restent sur votre téléphone. Seul le NIV vérifié est envoyé à la NHTSA. »

### 11. A mechanic reacts (Thursday)
- **Hook:** "I asked a mechanic what to check on a used car"
- **Shots:** a local mechanic (with permission) giving 3 tips → one tip is "check the
  recalls" → Car Report.
- **Why:** partners like this are the best word of mouth, and the mechanic gets free promotion.
- **Français:** same video with a French-speaking mechanic, or French subtitles.

### 12. Answer a comment (Saturday)
- Pick the best question from the comments this month ("Does it work for trucks?" "Does it
  show accidents?") and answer it on video, honestly. If the answer is "no, it doesn't show
  accidents", say where that data comes from (Carfax, ICBC, a lien search). Honesty builds trust.

## Flightboard (once it's in the stores, Sundays)

1. **"Picking someone up? Watch this."** Open an airport → Arrivals → find the flight →
   show the status and the **baggage belt**. Say: "Know exactly when to leave and where to wait."
2. **"What's flying over you right now"**: the **Live** tab radar near your home airport →
   tap a plane → its route. Avgeek audiences love this.
3. **"Gate changed? Don't run across the airport."** Departures → status, gate and
   terminal → the board refreshing on its own.
4. **"3,000+ airports in your pocket."** Search a small airport, then a huge one: both
   instant, because the airport list is built into the app.

**Caption ideas:** #avgeek #travelhacks #airport #planespotting #flighttracker
**Best timing:** before long weekends and holidays, when people search "flight status".
