Recalls, owner complaints and crash-test ratings from NHTSA, plus a logbook for your own service, odometer and registration records.

Your vehicles

    Where to find the VIN

    • DashboardLook through the windshield at the bottom corner on the driver's side.
    • Driver's door frameOn the sticker where the door latches.
    • RegistrationPrinted on your vehicle permit or ownership.
    • Insurance cardYour pink slip lists the VIN for each vehicle.

    A VIN has 17 characters and never uses the letters I, O or Q.

    What this report can't show

    Accident, lien and past-owner records come from paid sources such as Carfax Canada, ICBC in BC, or a provincial lien search. They aren't available here. You can keep your own service, odometer and registration history in My records.

    Vehicle details, U.S. recalls, owner complaints and crash ratings come from NHTSA, which receives the VIN you enter. If you add a MarketCheck key, MarketCheck receives the VIN when you ask for a listing history. Recalls are listed by model, so confirm what applies to a specific vehicle with a dealer. Not affiliated with NHTSA, Transport Canada, MarketCheck or Carfax.

    Your records, the VINs you check and your MarketCheck key, if you add one, are saved on this device only.
